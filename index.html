What Muvora Books is

Ten books. One horror story.
Then a brand new nightmare.

Most horror fiction gives you one novel and leaves you hanging until the sequel drops two years later. Muvora Books does the opposite. Every arc is a complete horror story told across ten short books, released one at a time, every Friday.

By book ten, the story is over. The killer is named. The house is empty. The thing in the woods is finished with you — or you're finished with it.

Then Arc 02 begins. New town, new monster, new nightmare. Nothing carries over except the feeling that follows you into the kitchen at 2 a.m.

Book 1 of every arc is free. If it doesn't keep you up, no one is asking you to pay. If it does — the other nine books are waiting.

Before you start

What the stories deal with

Muvora Books writes horror fiction. Stories may involve violence, death, disturbing imagery, and psychological dread. Nothing is gratuitous, but nothing is softened either. If a specific arc contains sensitive material, it will say so on its own page. All characters and events are fictional.
